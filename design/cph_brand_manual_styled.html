M3SH Core Values

Core Values

We strictly stand by the 3 C’s of CPH:

Curated (Quality & Exclusivity)

We are committed to achieve excellence in everything we do - maintaining strict vetting and onboarding standards for coaches and academies. We limit our pre-launch rollout to a core founding group of 100 select providers.

Connected (Trust & Agency)

We place special emphasis on bypassing administrative friction and operational hurdles, allowing parent and athlete communities to connect and transact directly with coaches.

Compliant (Privacy & Security)

We craft designs and backend architectures thoughtfully in order to promote absolute database security, strict zero-PII storage compliance, and direct secure database access.

M3SH Logo Concept

Logo Concept

The logo shows a clean geometric hub integrated within the structural lines of "CPH". The form depicts dynamism and connection, and the colors represent the tech-athletic focus of the brand.

The sports marketplace will be used by trainers, parent groups, and youth athletes of varying backgrounds, and this logo aims to capture that diversity and energy in a singular, cohesive hub mark. The ascending lines symbolize peak performance and progressive development.

M3SH Section 1.1: Persona

Section 1.1: Brand Voice & Persona

Our brand voice represents elite athletics coupled with data security. It is structured around four pillars:

1. Vetted & Elite

All copies must highlight high standards, curating select athletic trainers and professional cohort benchmarks.

2. Direct & Frictionless

We avoid complex operational overhead. The focus is on connecting athlete parents directly to trainers.

3. Security-First

We reassure users about direct private databases, zero PII retention, and complete backend control.

4. Athletic Precision

Our message is performance-driven and focused. Trust the training, own the game.

M3SH Section 1.2: Chatbot

Section 1.2: Chatbot Widget Branding

The chatbot acts as the "Interactive Tour Guide" for pre-launch users. Its design reflects the CPH identity guidelines:

Floating Trigger Button

Positioned bottom-right of the screen. Utilizes the CPH gradient text lockup and Neon Cyan glow effect.

Chat Window Styling

  • Background: Glassmorphic design using backdrop-blur filter on white background (light theme).
  • Headers: Slate-900 background with Neon Cyan logo emblem.
  • Typography: Poppins Light (300) for incoming text, Poppins Medium (500) for user controls.

Lead Onboarding Flow

Conversations must guide users organically to capture waitlist information (Name, Email, Mobile, Location, and User Role).

M3SH Usage Rules 1

Do’s and Don’ts

The logo represents the whole brand and the company. Always maintain certain visual standards to prevent brand erosion:

✓ Approved Proportions

Always maintain standard structural proportions and scale values of the logo.

✗ Never Distort or Rotate

Never stretch or play with proportions. Never tilt/rotate/flip the logo.
