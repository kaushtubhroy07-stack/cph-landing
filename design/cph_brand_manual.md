# Core Values
## The 3 C’s of CPH
*   **Curated (Quality & Exclusivity):** We maintain strict standards of excellence. Only vetted, elite coaches and academies are invited to list on the platform, limited to a pre-launch cohort of 100 select providers.
*   **Connected (Trust & Agency):** We bypass operational friction, allowing parents and athlete families to connect, schedule, and book directly with athletic trainers.
*   **Compliant (Privacy & Security):** Powered by M3SH's data architecture, we enforce zero-PII retention policies and secure backups on Salomon's private database.

---

## --- PAGE 3 ---
# Mission
Providing a secure, trusted, and highly curated space for elite youth sports trainers to scale their booking yield and connect directly with parent/athlete communities.

# Vision
Striving to elevate the standards of youth sports coaching by putting administrative operations on autopilot, allowing coaches to focus entirely on training and mentorship.

## --- PAGE 7 ---
# The Gradient
The brand gradient represents energy, movement, and growth. It transitions from CPH Elite White to M3SH Neon Cyan. It is a core visual element used across website headers, chatbot widget buttons, and digital marketing materials.
*   **CSS Definition:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #00E5FF 100%)`

# The Tagline
## Trust the Training. Own the Game.

---

## --- PAGE 9 ---
# Tagline Application
The tagline is used as a lockup underneath the wordmark or positioned independently on visual assets. Always maintain the standard proportions and spacing relative to the wordmark.
*   **Lockup Style:** Tagline should be set in Poppins Light, uppercase, with 0.15em letter spacing.

---

## --- PAGE 10 ---
# Typeface: Logotype
The wordmark logotype is to be set in one of the three approved typography options:
*   **Space Grotesk** (Recommended): A modern geometric sans-serif that projects a clean, tech-forward optimization.
*   **Montserrat**: A classic geometric typeface representing stability, trust, and professional athletic coaching.
*   **Outfit**: Sleek geometric curves that feel premium, modern, and family-oriented.

---

## --- PAGE 11 ---
# Typeface: Site & App
*   **Poppins:** Poppins will be used as the primary interface typeface on the website, landing pages, and in-app dashboard views. We prefer Poppins Light (300) for body text and Poppins Semi-Bold (600) for headers.
*   **JetBrains Mono:** JetBrains Mono is the secondary monospace font used for technical metrics, scheduling timetables, and coordinate telemetry.

---

## --- PAGE 12 ---
# Logo Symbol
The CPH logo symbol (icon) can be used independently to represent the brand without the wordmark text. It should however be used only on high-contrast white or dark slate backgrounds.

---

## --- PAGE 13 ---
# Space Around
Always leave sufficient breathing space around the logo and symbol. The minimum clear space must be equal to the height of the letter "C" in the wordmark. Never crowd it with adjacent text, patterns, or borders.

---

## --- PAGE 14 ---
# Alternate Logos
Monochrome alternate logos (pure black and pure white) are acceptable when background colors or print limitations make the gradient logo infeasible.

---

## --- PAGE 15 ---
# Logo Application
The monochrome white symbol should be used on dark photos or busy backgrounds, positioned in areas of high-contrast negative space to ensure readability.

## --- PAGE 17 ---
# Interactive Tour Guide Bot
The waitlist assistant acts as a friendly, professional "Interactive Tour Guide." It introduces parent and coach communities to CPH's direct booking model and features:
*   **Tone:** Welcoming, helpful, sports-knowledgeable, and conversion-focused.
*   **Aesthetics:** Transparent glassmorphic chat container with Neon Cyan border highlights and white text elements.
*   **Lead Capture Flow:** Progressively requests Name, Email, Mobile, Location, and Role (Coach/Parent/Athlete).

## --- PAGE 18 ---
# Logo Size (Print)
To maintain structural clarity in print media:
*   **Minimum Logo Symbol Width:** 1.0 inch wide.
*   **Minimum Full Logo Width:** 1.5 inches wide.
*   **Minimum Wordmark Text Width:** 0.5 inches wide.

---

## --- PAGE 19 ---
# Logo Size (Screen)
To maintain legibility on digital screens:
*   **Minimum Logo Symbol Width:** 60 pixels wide.
*   **Minimum Full Logo Width:** 100 pixels wide.
*   **Minimum Wordmark Text Width:** 32 pixels wide.

---

## --- PAGE 20 ---
# Do’s and Don’ts: Proportions
*   **Do** keep the proportions of the symbol and wordmark locked.
*   **Don't** stretch, squash, or skew the logo in any direction.
*   **Don't** tilt, rotate, or flip the logo mark.

---

## --- PAGE 21 ---
# Do’s and Don’ts: Style
*   **Don't** make patterns or design elements out of the logo symbol.
*   **Don't** alter the brand gradient colors or change the approved colors.
*   **Don't** use unapproved typefaces for the wordmark or body text.
*   **Don't** add drop shadows, stroke outlines, or glows to the logo symbol.
