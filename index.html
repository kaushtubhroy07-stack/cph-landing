OUR STORY: BUILT BY DMV SOCCER PARENTS

Dear DMV Soccer Families,

When our daughter Morgan started competitive soccer, we were stunned by how fragmented and overwhelming the youth soccer landscape was for families. Between club practices, travel logistics, and tournament schedules, we quickly realized that clubs only handle one part of the equation.

To truly help young athletes develop—and stay healthy, confident, and resilient—parents are left searching through word-of-mouth recommendations for technical coaches, speed and agility trainers, physical therapists, sports psychologists, and nutritionists.

We built The Complete Player Hub to give parents and youth players direct, immediate access to a curated network of dedicated coaches, specialists, and training businesses across all 6 core pillars of player development—plus college recruitment specialists, NIL and branding coaches, and international tournament and training opportunities. Whether your athlete is building foundational movement, sharpening game IQ, recovering from an injury, or preparing for college showcases, CPH brings the entire ecosystem into one trusted place.

"Our goal is simple: to bring clarity, access, and structure to youth soccer so every player can develop into a complete athlete — from the inside out."

Sol & Heather Salinas
Co-Founders, The Complete Player Hub
